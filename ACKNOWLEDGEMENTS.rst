Acknowledgements
================

This project would not exist without the people and organizations that built Gramine, its
Intel TDX and virtual-machine fork gramine-tdx, and the Tirith work this tree continues. We are
grateful to all of them. The copyright notices of every one of these authors are retained in the
source files.

Projects this tree builds on
----------------------------

- `Gramine <https://github.com/gramineproject/gramine>`__ (formerly *Graphene*): the library OS, the
  Linux and SGX PALs, the manifest tooling and the test suites.
- `gramine-tdx <https://github.com/gramineproject/gramine-tdx>`__: the Intel TDX backend and the
  virtual-machine (``vm``) PAL, whose full history this repository keeps.
- Tirith: the virtual-machine and graphics host-redirection work developed at the ASTERISC Research
  Lab (see ``Documentation/devel/tirith-delta.md``).
